# LOVELEEDAY brand colors (owner's palette, out of scope for design review)

Extracted 2026-10-11 from the live stylesheets (`public/site/assets/site.css`, `pub.css`, `chrome.css`, `notes.css`) and the computed colors in `ai-tells-2026-10-11/crawl/all.json` (35 pages).
