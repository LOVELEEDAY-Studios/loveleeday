Fourteen ways the site reads as AI-made

Each pair shows the live pattern on the left and a proposed fix on the right, in LOVELEEDAY's own colors (white, ink, blue, cream, dark) with Switzer and Sentient and real photography. Under each pair is the section from a company site it was benchmarked against. Counts come from a crawl of 35 live pages on Oct 11. Click any side for full size.

The ten biggest tells, by count

  1. Default system font stack100 percent of 182,485 characters
  2. Tracked all-caps labels562 across 35 pages, 42 on the home page, 85 percent at 10px
  3. Two-tone headlines46 percent of h1 and h2
  4. Two-part headlines ("Short. Short.")54 of 185 headings, 8 on the home page
  5. No type scale13 distinct h2 sizes, 98 percent at one weight
  6. Pill chips that carry no state351
  7. Rounded surfaces in identical rows255 surfaces, 132 (52 percent) in rows of 3 or more

1Two-part, two-tone headline

Count on the live site
54 of 185 h1/h2 two-part; 46 percent two-tone
What it is
Two short sentences, the second in gray. Same device on 54 of 185 headings.
Cited source
S1 generic slogans; S2 accenting one headline part; S11 two fragments glued together

Benchmarked against

2Tracked all-caps label above every heading

Count on the live site
562 labels, mean 16 per page, 42 on home
What it is
10px uppercase labels with wide tracking on every section. 562 across 35 pages, 85 percent at 10px.
Cited source
S5 excessive letter spacing; S2 tracked all-caps eyebrows over every heading

Benchmarked against

3System default font stack

Count on the live site
100 percent of 182,485 characters in the system stack
What it is
Every character on the site is set in -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Helvetica Neue, Arial: the browser default, so the type carries no choice of its own.
Cited source
S8 stock tokens are a palette; S14 SF license and Apple-clone note; typography-mastery SF Pro entries

Benchmarked against

4No type scale: 13 heading sizes, one weight

Count on the live site
13 distinct h2 sizes; 98 percent weight 500
What it is
h2 sizes: 26, 30, 34, 36, 38, 40, 44, 48, 50, 52, 60, 62 px; 98 percent at weight 500 with -0.045em tracking.
Cited source
S12 arbitrary sizes, everything equally important; S13 modular scale, two to three weights

Benchmarked against

5Product card pasted over the hero photo

Count on the live site
home hero, portal sign-in, snapshot
What it is
A floating white card of four tracked labels sits across the photo and hides her hands; the same card repeats on the portal sign-in.
Cited source
S5 decoration cosplaying as information; S7 uniform sections; our crop rule (no overlap on faces or hands)

Benchmarked against

6Three equal columns with a glyph on top

Count on the live site
home "what you get"; operating-system, product page
What it is
Rule, tiny icon, title, two lines of gray text, three times across. The icons are 14px characters.
Cited source
S1 three feature cards; S7 three equal feature cards, icon-title-two-lines

Benchmarked against

7Four identical dark cards with category tag and chips

Count on the live site
home, architecture, industries (255 rounded surfaces, 52 percent in identical rows)
What it is
Same radius, border, padding, label, question, chips and status pill, four times.
Cited source
S1/S4/S7 identical card grids; S2 card kit; S3 uniform layout

Benchmarked against

8Pill chips that carry no state

Count on the live site
351 pill-shaped chips across the site
What it is
351 pills: tags, status dots, "Illustrative example" badges, "Talk to us" capsules. A green dot beside "One customer" is not a status.
Cited source
S5 generic Live/New badges, decoration cosplaying as information

Benchmarked against

9Glowing multicolor network brain

Count on the live site
home hero-adjacent stage, product page, operating-system (brain.js canvas)
What it is
A rotating mesh of green, blue, orange and purple nodes on black. This is the stock "AI brain" vocabulary.
Cited source
S16 stock listings for glowing neural brains; S1/S4 glow with no reason. The brand idea (records connecting) stays; the rendering changes.

Benchmarked against

10Fake browser window around a mock UI

Count on the live site
home interactive preview, operating-system
What it is
Three gray traffic-light dots, a breadcrumb and a "preview" label wrap a hand-built interface; pills and a sparkle glyph decorate it.
Cited source
S8 template screenshot genre; S5 decoration cosplaying as information (our judgment; no source names browser chrome)

Benchmarked against

11Copy built from stock frames

Count on the live site
18 not-X-it's-Y frames; two-part headline on 29 pages
What it is
"The problem isn't X. It's Y." and "It isn't A. It's B." open 18 sentences across the notes essays; one-line example disclaimers repeat under nearly every figure.
Cited source
S11 not-X-it's-Y, two fragments, always three things; S10 predictable rhythm is the real tell; S2 delete any number you cannot source

Benchmarked against

12Every section is the same two-column split

Count on the live site
72 sections at 96/96 padding; split pattern on most pages
What it is
Label, two-tone headline left, one gray paragraph right, 96px above and below, alternating dark, white, gray and cream bands. 72 sections share one 96/96 padding.
Cited source
S7 uniform sections, same height padding and heading size; S8 visibly undecided; S3 uniform layout

Benchmarked against

13An arrow on every row, a link with an arrow under it

Count on the live site
27 arrow glyphs in copy plus one per list row
What it is
Eight single-line rows each ending in a diagonal arrow, then "Explore your industry" and "Explore the work" with more arrows.
Cited source
S7 chevron CTAs on every link; S2 arrow on every link

Benchmarked against

14Portal home: checklist, empty card, blue links

Count on the live site
portal.loveleedaystudios.com home (screenshot qa_client_1440); cards centered, links #0066cc
What it is
Eyebrow plus big tight heading, a "Getting started" rounded card with struck-through steps in default blue, then a centered empty state in a second identical card.
Cited source
S3 missing unhappy paths and empty states that prompt the next action; S7 missing states; S2 card kit
