Most alerts watch events. The useful ones watch conditions.

A late shipment is an event. A late shipment for the one customer whose contract has a penalty clause is a condition. Event alerts fire hundreds of times a week; a condition fires when something is actually wrong.

A migration is finished when someone can ask last year's question and get last year's answer, not when the last row lands.

Give each old name an alias that points to the one record, and keep where the name came from.
