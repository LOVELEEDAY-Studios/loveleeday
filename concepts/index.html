Micruity — portfolio company rebuild

Light register — current
Rebuilt off-white in the healthtech register (reference: Ease, Headspace Health, Biograph via Mobbin). Photography regenerated on film-stock prompts with environmental framing — the tight, glowing portraits were the AI tell. Structured on Micruity’s own page spine — Overview, Benefits, How it Works, Get Started — the sub-nav pattern every one of their Solutions pages uses. Badges moved from text chips at 92% page depth to a trust bar under the hero. Photography generated IMAGE-TO-IMAGE on nano-banana from a licensed photographic source, not text-to-image. Five text-to-image rounds across two engines all read as fake; the banked finding says photoreal needs a real source frame, and that turned out to be the whole answer.

People-led — current
Hero is a participant, not a cable. Sections follow Micruity's own published Solutions taxonomy — MARS, Plan Participant Portability, Multi-Insurer TDF, Recordkeeper — and the CB Insights, SOC 2 and Drata badges are carried. Portraits are generated editorial, captioned as situations and never as named customers.

Full landing page — complete
Seven sections, no two adjacent sharing a silhouette: photo hero, type-only statement, dense routing table, asymmetric developer split with real curl, trust row where each claim carries its check, people and logos as marked placeholders, centred close.

Steel cable — photographic — current
Generated hero used as the ground. The headline says "the wire between recordkeepers and insurers" and the object is a steel cable. Third generation: the most photographic variants came back frayed and rusting — wrong message for financial infrastructure — so the prompt had to specify brand new, every filament flush, no broken strands.

Lit perspective — routing fabric in depth
One light source upper-right, panels at different Z, vignette and grain. Drama from light rather than rotation; the base plane bleeds right but no figure gets cut.

First pass — flat
