02 // Our Technology

Tunable Nanocellulose
Industrial Performance

While nanocellulose is a powerful material platform, mass-market adoption has historically been blocked by the high cost and chemical intensity required to tune it for real-world applications. Our proprietary cellulose treatment technology overcomes this barrier, delivering chemically tuned, functionalized nanofibers at industrial scale—without the traditional cost and complexity.

Plug-and-play tunability

Customizable chemical and physical properties for drop-in performance.

03 // Specs

Material Properties

Nanocellulose is not a single material — it is a tunable nanofiber platform. By controlling how these fibers are processed and assembled, we engineer a new class of additives that deliver multiple performance functions at once.

Request data sheet →
01/05
High Strength, Low Density
Up to 8× stronger than steel by weight while remaining lighter than Kevlar. As an additive, it enhances the strength of bulk materials but can also improve bonding between layers in composite systems.
Tensile Strength: 22 GPa
02/05
New Class of Binding Chemistry
Forms a highly complex 3D network, making for an effective binder at ultra-low concentrations — a next-generation binder for filters, composites, and other advanced material systems.
Solids Required: <1%
03/05
Barrier Properties
With its dense nanofiber network, our nano additive forms an effective barrier against gases, oils, and moisture — extending shelf life and providing environmental protection.
Film Forming Ability: High
04/05
Exceptional Water Retention
Forms a porous network that can hold more than 200 times its weight in water — useful from drought-resistant agriculture to industrial coatings and filtration.
Water Holding: 200× weight
05/05
Superior Particle Dispersion
Creates a stable network that evenly distributes and holds other additives in place, enabling high-performance hybrid formulations.
Solids to Disperse: <1%
