The moat

The proprietary experimental data NovaLab generates trains NovaEngine on real biological feedback — which is why no external dataset can replicate it, and why the gap widens with every cycle.

In-house

Most platforms stop at the prediction. Ours has a bench behind it.

NovaLab is our own wet lab, not a vendor queue. Candidates NovaEngine designs are synthesized, run in cells and characterized here — which is why the result comes back in weeks and why the data is ours to train on.

That is the whole loop in one sentence: the lab is the reason the model keeps improving, and the model is the reason the lab is worth running.
