The loop

The tools that exist today predict. Ours design — then check the answer.

Traditional RNA development relies on iterative screening, trial-and-error synthesis and expensive wet lab cycles that can take years to yield a viable candidate. Novarna closes the cycle, and every turn of it makes the next one better.

01 — Design

NovaEngine generates

Entirely novel RNA sequences optimized for a target from the ground up, not screened from a library.

02 — Build

NovaLab synthesises

Mapping and synthesis of the AI-generated candidates in-house.

03 — Test

Functional validation

Cellular assays, in vitro experimentation, pooled screening and structural characterisation.

04 — Learn

The data returns

Every experiment becomes training signal. The platform gets better every time it works.

This is the moat. The proprietary experimental data NovaLab generates trains NovaEngine on real biological feedback — which is why no external dataset can replicate it, and why the gap widens with every cycle.
