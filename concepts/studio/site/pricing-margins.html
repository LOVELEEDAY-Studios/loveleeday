The price is only the beginning

Follow the money.
Item by item.

A listed price can look healthy while discounts and delivery costs tell a different story. Connect the price a customer actually pays with the costs attached to that sale.

A margin review makes those assumptions explicit. It identifies prices to investigate and keeps proposed changes tied to their inputs.
