04b · Gradients

Built from the palette, not bought.

Twelve stock gradients were pulled and looked at; nine were the purple-magenta AI-wallpaper register. These are mesh gradients composed from this palette’s own hues, so they move when a token moves. A gradient is a stage ground, never a fill behind small text, and each is measured at its LIGHTEST stop rather than its average — which is how the first version shipped teal at 4.29:1 on dawn.
