Operational intelligence

See the connections.
Find the next question.

Bring people, companies, items, documents, and places into a shared context. Follow a change to the decisions it could affect.

A system for the questions between systems

The answer crosses
more than one team.

A supplier change can affect a catalogue, a location, and a forecast. A recurring service issue can connect a property, a contractor, and a spending pattern.

Operational intelligence begins by defining those relationships. It gives a cross-system question an evidence trail and a scope someone can review.
