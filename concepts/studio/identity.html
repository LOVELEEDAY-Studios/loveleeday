Identity · one mark, one wordmark, one lockup

The heart is the system.

Not a heart with a network drawn on top of it — that was tried, twenty-four times, and it reads as a tangle at full size and a smudge at sixteen pixels. The heart is assembled. It is a single silhouette divided into modules by channels, so the parts are visible and the outline never breaks. The same heart, at a fifth of the size, is the counter inside the O.

02 · The wordmark

Real outlines, measured spacing, one letter redrawn.

Manrope 800 — the face the site already sets its headlines in. The O keeps the typeface’s own outer contour; only its counter is replaced. Spacing is not a table of guessed numbers: the facing ink profiles of every adjacent pair are sampled across their shared height and the advance solved for a constant average gap, with a floor that makes a collision impossible.
