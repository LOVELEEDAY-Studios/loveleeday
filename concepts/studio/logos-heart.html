LOVELEEDAY Studios · identity · the heart brief · 2026-09-21

The heart is
the system.

Your brief, and it is a better one than the abstract set: the heart carries the name and the connected systems carry the product, so the mark says both halves of the company at once. The whole difficulty is that a heart is read from its silhouette — two lobes and a point — and every node, gap and trace added to it is an attack on that silhouette. So each of these was tested at 16px before it was refined, not after. Geometry is real: two circles of equal radius, centres one radius apart, on a square rotated 45°. No freehand curves.

Vector, not raster — which is what a mark has to be. Midjourney is running the same brief in parallel for metaphor and silhouette exploration; anything it finds would still have to be redrawn as vector to be usable, so this track is not waiting on it.
