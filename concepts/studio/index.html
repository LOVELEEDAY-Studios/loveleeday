Four ways to look
like a big company.

Each of these is a different answer to the same question, and they differ in what the page is looking at — not in palette. Two directions that differ only in colour are one direction. Built from captures of apple.com, palantir.com/platforms/foundry, nvidia.com and a Bloomberg terminal taken today. Every object on every page is drawn in canvas: no stock photography, and nothing licensed. Click any card to open it full size.

These are mockups, not builds. Copy is real where it can be and illustrative where it is labelled. The live site currently runs a fifth direction, which is the one being replaced.
