Ten ways to look like a big company.

Each of these answers the same question differently, and they differ in what the page is looking at — not in palette. Two directions that differ only in colour are one direction. 01–04 were built from captures of apple.com, palantir.com/platforms/foundry, nvidia.com and a Bloomberg terminal; 05–10 add an annual report, a network console, a consulting case file, a product spec, a service catalogue and an essay. Every ground is light — dark was rejected — and each one carries the type and colour system its way of looking implies, because a palette on a swatch card is a paint chip, not a concept. Every object is drawn: no stock photography, nothing licensed, and the only photographs are ours. Click any card to open it full size.

These are mockups, not builds. Copy is real where it can be and illustrative where it is labelled. The live site currently runs direction 02.
