LOVELEEDAY · landing page concepts

Five ways to update it.

Every one has the cycling photographic hero, the dropdown navigation, Bloomberg-register furniture, and the brain in place of the internal dashboard. They differ in which of those leads.

01

Terminal

Bloomberg at full strength. Amber on black, a running ticker, four-letter function codes, dense panels. Photography is confined to one small rail, because the Terminal is not a photo medium.

02

Dispatch

Photography leads: full-bleed, cycling, Ken Burns. The data rail sits ON the image rather than under it, and the brain gets its own black band below the fold.

03

Split

The fold is divided. Photograph on the left, the brain running LIVE on the right, so the first thing on the page is the product thinking.

04

Exchange

Bloomberg in daylight. White ground, amber accents, dense tables, the photograph reduced to one framed panel. The brain band is the only dark moment and it lands harder for it.

05

Signal

A twelve-cell mosaic where every cell changes photograph on its own offset, so the wall never re-tiles in step. Almost no furniture: the brain is the single centrepiece.
