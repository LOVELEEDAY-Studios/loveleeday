LOVELEEDAY Studios · identity study · 2026-09-21

Ten marks, one grid.

Every one is drawn on the same 100×100 grid, on 10-unit steps, with stroke weights between 9 and 11 units — which is what makes them a family rather than ten drawings. All are flat, single-colour and tested at sixteen pixels, because a mark that has only been seen large has not been tested. Each card shows the mark at 96px, at 24px, at 16px, reversed out of navy, and locked up with the wordmark.

Researched before drawn. Dribbble’s popular geometric-logo results are decorative monograms in rounded squares — portfolio work, not the register of a company preparing to be public. The benchmark marks and Brand New say the opposite: wordmark-led, one geometric mark, one colour. From knowledge/design/brand-mastery.md: Law 1, the brand is a feeling — the one this company must own is knowing where a number came from; and Law 2, evolution strengthens where disruption resets, which is why mark 01 evolves the existing 2×2 grid rather than discarding its equity.

None of these is live. The site currently runs the existing mark. Tell me a number and I will cut the full set — favicon, app icon, OG lockup, reversed and one-colour variants, and the clear-space rule.
