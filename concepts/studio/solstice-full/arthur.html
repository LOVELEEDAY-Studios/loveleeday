Arthur — intelligence architecture

Designed to act.

Arthur’s codebase brings together the foundations for remembering context, resolving who and what a record refers to, coordinating work, selecting models, and acting through tools.

These are implemented architectural components, not a claim that every component runs in every workflow. We scope and validate the configuration for each engagement.

Fig. 03 — a 5×5×5 lattice, lit and depth-sorted in the browser. No 3D library.

Context that can outlast a conversation.

Journals, events and objects are stored and indexed. Retrieval makes what was stored available to the execution paths that use it, so a project carries its own history rather than being re-briefed each time.

Project historyMemory fabricRetrieved context

Projects need continuity. Prior decisions and constraints should inform the next piece of work. Recall coverage and integration vary by execution path.

Why it matters

Four names, one company.

A payments customer, a vendor id in the ledger, a string in an email signature and a line in a PDF are four records and one object. Resolution collapses them, so a question is asked about the object rather than about whichever string a source happened to use.

Source recordsResolverOne object

Every figure that aggregates across systems is wrong until this step exists. Coverage is exactly as wide as the connected sources, and no wider.

Why it matters

What was true, and what we knew.

Each property carries when it was valid in the world and when it was observed. You can ask what was true on a date, or what was known on a date — two different questions that most systems collapse into one.

Valid fromObserved atAs known at

Judging an August decision with September knowledge is look-ahead bias, and it is the default mistake. Making the two timelines separate is what stops it happening quietly.

Why it matters

A number with no trail is not reportable.

Writing a property requires the source system and the source reference. The trail can be printed for any value, back to the record it came from.

Source systemSource referenceFull trail

This is mechanical claim verification rather than a promise to be careful. The refusal is in the write path, so it cannot be forgotten under time pressure.

Why it matters

Execution that has to prove it ran.

Models route to the work, tools execute against real systems, and results are checked against an observed proof string before anything is reported as done.

RouteExecuteObserve proof

An HTTP 200 is not evidence that the thing you asked for happened. Work closes on a value read back out of the system that was supposed to change.

Looking closer

Arthur is an intelligence software architecture. Its components surround model reasoning with persistent memory, task scheduling, tools, routing, and evaluation. Models are part of the system; the configuration determines which capabilities a workflow can use.

We assess your data, APIs, permissions, and requirements before committing to an integration. An adapter in the architecture does not mean a live connection to your systems already exists.

Custom applications, workflow tools, research and decision support, digital experiences, and connected creative deliverables. We agree on a specific scope and acceptance criteria around the business problem.
