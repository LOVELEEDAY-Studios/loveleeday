LOVELEEDAY Studios · colour & typography study · 2026-09-21

Four systems. No dark grounds.

The dark ground in the last round was my assumption, not the references’. Apple’s product pages sit on #F5F5F7, Palantir Foundry is white, Oracle is white — dark is a stage those companies cut to for one section, never the whole site. Nothing below has a dark ground. Each system differs in category, not in shade: a warm institutional one, a Swiss one, an editorial one and an enterprise one. The applied fragment on the right of each block is identical every time, so the only thing changing is colour and type.

Sourced from knowledge/design/color-palette-mastery.md — §“LOVELEEDAY Studios (Recommended System)”, which System A implements; §“Mistake 2: Saturated Colors on Large Surfaces”, which is why every ground sits at low chroma and full saturation only appears under about 40px; §“Mistake 3: Pure Black as Text”, which is why no ink here is #000. Pairings from knowledge/design/typography-mastery.md §“50+ Proven Font Pairings”, one drawn from each of four different categories. Every contrast ratio below is computed in the browser from the hex beside it.

Contrast is measured with the WCAG 2.1 relative-luminance formula against both of the system’s own grounds. AA = 4.5:1, safe for body text at any size. AA-lg = 3:1, safe for text at 24px, or 18.66px bold, and for UI borders — not for paragraphs.
