# Hero standard

Every page opens with one of four hero types. A hero has one ground and at most one photograph; a second picture layer is a defect (`review/out/measure/hero` via `_hero.html` checks this).

| Type | Ground | Picture | Type colour | Card | Use on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Photo | night + 50% night scrim | one photograph, full bleed | white | answer card on the right, inside the hero, vertically centred | what we offer and who we are: home, studio, operating system, how it works, architecture, integrations, trust, every solution page, every industry page, work, build, principles, talk, contact, FAQ |
| Product | cream | the real portal screen in the standard screen frame, right | ink | none, the screen is the proof | Arthur product page only |
| Text | cream (legal) or night (notes) | none | ink / white | none | reading pages: notes, articles, privacy, terms, the industries index (tiles follow) |

Shared rules for every type
- h1 uses the brand display size, max 12ch; lead 17-22px, max 34em; one primary button plus one text link.
- Left text column starts on the 1080px wrap line; Photo and Product heroes are at least 620px tall on desktop; a Text hero is sized to its content (about 360-440px) and the same height on every Text page.
- On phones (700px and under) every photo hero stacks: photo, then text, then card, all full width; no text over a busy part of the photo.
- Photos are real, single shots at native pixels; never hero-decision.jpg, hero-team-desktop.jpg, hero-team-phone.jpg.
- Industry pages may replace the right card with the product panel across the bottom edge; they use it on all three industry pages or none.
