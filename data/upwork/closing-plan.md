# LOVELEEDAY on Upwork — closing plan (2026-09-24)

## The constraint
A brand-new agency with 0 reviews and a limited Connects budget. Each proposal costs 9–26 Connects (read from the job pages), so 150 Connects buys about 8 proposals. Every proposal has to go to a job we can realistically win.

## Goal for the first 7 days
Three signed fixed-price contracts and three 5-star reviews. Reviews, not revenue, are what unlock the larger work.

## Rule 1 — only bid where the odds are real
All must be true: fixed price · in our lane · 10 or fewer proposals · 0–1 already interviewing · client hire rate 50%+ · client has spent $1K+ · client active in the last 3 days · payment verified.

## Rule 2 — proof before the pitch
With no reviews, the proposal has to show the work. Before each bid, Arthur builds a small, specific proof for that job (a field map of their feed, the automation diagram, a sample of the dashboard or analysis) and the proposal leads with it.

## Rule 3 — speed and price
Apply within 2 hours of a job posting. Bid at the client's budget with a delivery date shorter than they asked for. For the first three contracts, a small discount is fine if it lowers the client's risk; never below the value of the review.

## Rule 4 — the profile has to close
Clients who click through need proof: add the 5 shipped portfolio items to the agency page and publish 2–3 fixed-price Project Catalog offers so buyers can come to us.

## Rule 5 — cadence and measurement
Scan twice a day (morning, evening) with upwork-job-scan.sh, check signals with upwork-job-detail.sh, send 3–5 proposals a day. Track each proposal: viewed, replied, interviewed, hired. After 10 proposals, drop the categories that do not convert.

## Rule 6 — then move up
After 3 reviews: raise prices and shift to the signature lane — pricing, margin and analytics work at $1,500–$5,000, where Daniel is strongest.
