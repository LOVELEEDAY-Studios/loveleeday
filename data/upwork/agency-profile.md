Prepared 2026-09-24. Everything below is paste-ready for the Upwork agency profile, built only from work LOVELEEDAY has actually shipped.

## Facts that shape the plan (checked 2026-09-24)
- Upwork money is not instant. Once a client approves (or auto-releases) a payment it sits in a **five-day security hold** as "Pending" before it can be withdrawn. After you submit fixed-price work, the client has up to **14 days** to review before it auto-releases. Source: Upwork Help, read through the Steel browser on 2026-09-24.
- On a brand-new account, the fastest path to money is small fixed-price jobs that a client can approve the same day.
- An agency is created from an owner's Upwork account (help index: "How to create an agency"). The owner's identity verification has to be done by Daniel personally.
- Upwork job pages sit behind a Cloudflare check that the Steel browser could not pass on 2026-09-24, so job hunting happens inside Daniel's logged-in account.

## Overview (paste into "Agency overview")
Most small and mid-size businesses already have the information they need. It is spread across a POS, a bank feed, an inbox, a spreadsheet and three tools that do not talk to each other. LOVELEEDAY Studios connects those systems and builds software that does the work.

We are a small studio that ships production software, not prototypes. Our own products run real businesses every day:
- **olldae**, a bar and restaurant operating system covering inventory, recipes, catering and billing.
- **Owwyl**, an AI bookkeeping product that reads a company's bank, card processor and bills inbox overnight, matches every bill to its payment and keeps real double-entry books.
- **A hospitality operations layer** that runs a working cocktail bar's email, scheduling, reporting and marketing.
- **Duezy**, invoice automation.
- **A secure client portal** with multi-factor sign-in, single sign-on, encrypted documents and audited access.

What we do for clients:
- **Custom applications:** internal tools, client portals and dashboards (Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase or Postgres, Stripe).
- **Workflow automation and AI:** connect your tools, remove the copy-and-paste, and add AI where it helps, with a human approving anything that moves money.
- **Data migration and cleanup:** QuickBooks, Xero, POS and spreadsheet data moved and reconciled so the numbers tie.
- **Websites:** fast, accessible and designed properly, not a template with the demo text still in it.
- **Website and systems audits:** a written, evidence-backed report of what is broken, slow or costing you customers, with fixes ranked by effort.

How we work: a fixed scope and a fixed price wherever possible, a working preview you can click early, and weekly progress you can see. You own the code and the data.
