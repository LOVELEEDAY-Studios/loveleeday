enFuse® On-Body Delivery Platform

Meet enFuse®

enFuse is an innovative, wearable drug delivery platform designed to comfortably and conveniently deliver large volumes of medications subcutaneously. Featuring hands‑free wearable technology, patients receive their needed treatment through a simple site injection under the skin — instead of intravenously.

02 · Our Technology

One platform, three systems — built so an IV bag becomes something a patient wears.

enFuse is not a single part. It is a transfer system that prepares the dose, a wearable that delivers it, and a smart layer that keeps the patient and the care team connected through the infusion — the three systems named on our own technology page.

01

enFuse Transfer Systems

Moves the medication from vial or prefilled syringe into the wearable's reservoir — the preparation step that happens before the device ever touches skin.

Preparation
02

enFuse Wearable

Adheres to the skin and delivers large volumes of medication subcutaneously, hands‑free, instead of an IV line — the device patients actually wear.

Delivery
03

enFuse Smart Technology

A companion digital layer — built with Aptar Digital Health — that tracks the infusion and keeps patients and providers connected to it in real time.

Connected care
