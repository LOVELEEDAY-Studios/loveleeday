Why Lightship

A light where there isn't one yet.

A lightship is a vessel that does a lighthouse's job in waters where no lighthouse can stand. Lightship Capital does the same for founders: it backs remarkable businesses in the places coastal funds don't look, and gives them the resources every startup needs to thrive.

Impact and profit are simultaneously achievable.
