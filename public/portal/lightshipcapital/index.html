01How we invest

Criteria, stated plainly.

Lightship Capital invests in remarkable businesses led by FOC (Founders of Color), LGBTQ+, Women & Innovators with Disabilities — supporting founders and their teams with the resources every startup needs to thrive.

Industries

Five sectors, as stated on lightship.capital/about.

CPGE-CommerceSustainabilityArtificial IntelligenceHealth Tech

Where

Companies based anywhere in the United States, excluding New York, Boston, and San Francisco. Companies based in the Midwest and South are the focus, and firms based in or willing to relocate to Ohio, Tulsa, OK or Arkansas are given additional consideration.

Stage

Post-Product, Post-Revenue, Pre-Seed to Series A.
