02 · What we do

We don't point you toward capital. We get you ready to reach it.

01

Training

Coaching that takes you from a pitch you are not sure about to one you can give from memory, and a clear read on which funding is worth your time and which is not.

02

Capital

A growing network of real sources: banks, alternative lenders, grant makers, angels and venture capital — matched to your business. If ACCESS Lab is not the right fit for you, we will still point you to the one that is.

03

Traction

Money in the account is not the finish line. Our preferred-vendor network covers marketing, operations and financial management, so what you raise turns into growth instead of sitting still.

03 · The program

VentureHue ACCESS Lab.

A capital readiness accelerator for early-stage technology founders. No equity taken, no fee to raise — the training, the network and the visibility it takes to get funded.

Built for underrepresented and overlooked pre-seed and seed-stage technology founders
Led by Brittni Abiolu, Program Manager of the Michigan Founders Fund Pre-Accelerator with gener8tor
Founders in the network have raised $2,192,470 to date
We also run our own angel syndicate, investing alongside our founders in pre-seed and seed rounds
01

Apply

Tell us what you are building and what you are trying to raise. It takes a few minutes and there is no cost to apply.

02

Review

We read every application and sit down with you to understand what you are actually up against, and whether this program is the right answer to it.

03

Approve

If it is a fit, you are in, and the readiness work starts. We keep cohorts small on purpose.

04

Raise

Training, the network and the visibility work run together until you are in front of the right source with a number you can defend.
