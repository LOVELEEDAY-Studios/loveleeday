What Rosie actually does

Six things your team stops doing on Monday morning.

These are the six problems your own demo lets a teacher pick from. Here is what happens after they pick one.

01 — Slow Wi-Fi

She checks the access point before anyone files a ticket.

Rosie reads live device telemetry, identifies whether the problem is the room, the device or the network, and either fixes it or hands your team a diagnosis instead of a complaint.

"Wi-Fi is slow in room 214." — and it is resolved before lunch.
02 — Broken hardware

Knowledge-aware escalation, so a human only gets what needs a human.

A cracked screen is not a password reset. Rosie recognises the difference, opens the right ticket in the system you already run, and routes it with the device history already attached.

The 40% she escalates arrive structured, not as "my computer is broken."
03 — Printers, apps, passwords

The routine 60%, handled without you.

Password resets, network diagnostics, print queues, apps that will not load. Rosie walks the teacher through it or does it outright, and closes the ticket herself.

Your queue gets shorter without anyone being hired.
04 — Phishing

The one where being fast matters most.

Staff can ask Rosie whether an email is real before they click it. That is a 30-second conversation instead of an incident report.

"Spot a phishing email" is a chip on your own demo. It should be a headline.
