02How it works

Heat does the separating. Nothing leaves as waste.

A steam-based process pulls clean water out of the stream and leaves the solids behind. The solids become feed and biochar. The water goes back into your wash cycle. The design reuses heat from its own power supply, which is where the efficiency comes from.

09Questions

What producers ask first.

01What kinds of waste can the system process?+

Spent wash, stillage and wastewater: the streams usually treated as a disposal cost. They become feed, fertilizer and clean water.

02How does this cut our disposal costs?+

Processing on site means no hauling, no tipping fees and no third-party treatment. You also end up with products to sell or reuse, or you can join the resale and profit-sharing program.

03What are dried distillers’ grains, and who buys them?+

High-protein, nutrient-rich livestock feed made from spent grain. Farmers and feed markets buy it, which gives you a direct revenue stream. It also has uses in bioreactors, bioplastics and hydroponics.

04What is phosphorus-enhanced biochar, and who wants it?+

A carbon-rich soil amendment packed with nutrients. Farms, vineyards and regenerative growers are looking for sustainable alternatives to synthetic fertilizer.

05Can it really make drinkable water from our wastewater?+

Yes. The patented process treats wastewater to potable standard, so it can be reused for cleaning, cooling or even bottling, depending on your application and local regulations.

06How does this help with environmental compliance?+

You discharge much less wastewater, which makes the standards easier to meet and fines easier to avoid. A circular process also strengthens your sustainability record.

07Does it work for small craft producers?+

Setups are modular and sized to your production, from a microbrewery to a major distillery. If space is tight, ask about the waste pickup service.

08How long until it pays for itself?+

Most customers see a return within 12 to 24 months, depending on waste volume and the resale value of what comes out.

09What support or maintenance does it need?+

It is designed for low-touch operation, with remote monitoring and full service support.

10How is this different from traditional treatment?+

Traditional treatment gets rid of waste. This turns it into products, so waste stops being a cost center and starts paying.
