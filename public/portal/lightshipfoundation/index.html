Lightship Bootcamp

Your first million is closer than you think.

A free, in-person program that brings entrepreneurs together for three focused days of learning, mentorship and growth. Lightship covers accommodations and meals, so founders can fully engage in refining their business strategy, financials and investor pitch.

Founder Gym

Where ideas become action.

An equity-free program combining expert training, proven frameworks and hands-on mentorship to help entrepreneurs build with clarity, confidence and purpose.

Apply to Founder Gym →

  1. Build your foundation

    Align your purpose with your idea, define your customer, and validate your problem and solution using frameworks like the Lean Canvas.

  2. Know your market

    Discover early adopters, uncover where your market is underserved, and shape an MVP that resonates with real customer needs.

  3. Master money & operations

    Learn smart pricing, understand your cost structure, and put simple systems in place so your business runs smoothly.

  4. Brand & launch

    Craft a brand that reflects your vision, build an actionable launch plan, and gather real feedback to iterate on.

Why we exist

Building a more
inclusive innovation economy.

We help founders build sustainable companies, generate wealth, and reinvest in their communities — because entrepreneurship is one of the most effective tools for building resilient communities. Six things we hold ourselves to: community first, creativity as a catalyst, bold innovation, sustainable impact, excellence over everything, and joy in the work.
