Elemental Media. Five directions.

Five different directions for the same studio, all built from Elemental’s own films, copy, people and contacts. Each keeps the colored client logos and plays on the four elements in its own way. Every film sits in one 2.39 frame. Open each at full size; they work on phones too.

CONCEPT 01

Light Table

Their cream, navy and rose, grown up into an editorial film magazine. Frames laid out like stills on a light table, each tagged with its element. Keeps their line, “Let’s tell the **** out of it.”

Projection

Slate

A production slate and call sheet. Big film titles in a typographic index, each with its element code (Fi, Wa, Ea, Ai); hovering reveals the frame. The most national-house of the five.

Elements

Crew
