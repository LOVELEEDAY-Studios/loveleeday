Pitch Night

The quickest pitch for capital in Southwest Michigan. The community votes five Kalamazoo businesses onto the stage; they pitch three judges for five minutes.

  • $10,000 equity-free to the winner
  • Every other month, 6 to 8 PM
  • Customers, partners and free resources for every finalist

Residency

A full-time, in-house year for companies with at least a year of traction, to find product-market fit and get investment-ready.

  • Introductions to at least 50 investors
  • Prepare to raise up to $1M
  • Product, marketing, sales and operations support
