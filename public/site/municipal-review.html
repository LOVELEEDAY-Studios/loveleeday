Review every ordinance on a municipality’s books.

We organize the full code of ordinances, trace each amendment to its source, and flag the questions that need a reviewer.

A useful review shows what applies and what changed.

Every review starts with a defined jurisdiction, a collection of source documents and a record of what is missing. We begin with the code, then connect the amendments, repeals and supporting materials.

The result is a cited inventory that shows the history of each provision and the questions that call for legal interpretation.

A review moves through three stages.

We establish coverage

We list the document sources, versions, jurisdiction and known gaps in the collection.

We follow the changes

We connect amendments, repeals, superseded provisions and cross-references to the documents that support them.

We prepare the review

We produce a cited register of issues, with the source passages and the questions for the responsible reviewer.

Public decisions need a record that can withstand scrutiny.

Each document and decision in the register links back to the source that supports it.
