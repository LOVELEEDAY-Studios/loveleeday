Our principles

Clear thinking.
Visible foundations.

The standards we want the system to carry into every answer, every record, and every decision.

The principles

01

Evidence before confidence.

A clear answer should make its support easier to inspect. Preserve the source record and distinguish an observed fact from an inference.

02

Context before conclusions.

A value belongs to a subject, a time, and a situation. Keep those relationships attached so the number can be understood.

03

History stays intact.

New information should add to the record. Preserve what was true and what was known when a decision was made.

04

Uncertainty stays visible.

Missing records, disputed values, and incomplete coverage should be part of the answer. Give the reviewer a useful next question.

05

People keep the decision.

A proposed action needs a clear scope and a responsible owner. Make the consequences and supporting evidence available before a consequential change.

06

The work should return time.

The purpose of connecting information is a clearer decision and fewer repeated reconciliations. More room for attention is the outcome we care about.

How we work
