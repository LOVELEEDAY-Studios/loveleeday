Intelligence is designed.
Trust is built in.

Five connected layers give information identity, history, provenance, and a standard of evidence.

Explore the layers

Identity

Different names.
One subject.

A company might be a familiar name in a conversation, a customer ID in a payment system, and a supplier record in a ledger. The ontology connects those references to a single subject.

Connect the names without losing the source identifiers. Keep uncertain matches available for review.

In practiceHES → Harbor Equipment Services → a canonical company record.

Lineage

Every fact.
A way back.

An observation carries the source system and record that support it. That trail gives someone reviewing an answer a place to begin.

A missing source is a gap in the evidence. It should remain visible instead of being hidden behind confident wording.

In practiceObservation → source system → source reference.

Memory

What was true.
What was known.

The bitemporal record separates when a fact held in the world from when it became known to the system. That distinction preserves the context of a past decision.

A later correction adds information. It does not rewrite what someone could have known earlier.

In practiceThe supplied payment example keeps the earlier $902.58 balance and the later $0.00 balance.

Verification

A claim needs
something behind it.

The verifier checks figures against recorded properties. An unsupported figure stays out of the answer until evidence supports it.

A polished sentence and a supported statement are separate things. Verification connects the statement to a named subject and recorded value.

In practiceThe supplied “4,000 objects resolved” claim is flagged as unsupported.

Awareness

When facts change,
attention follows.

Standing conditions describe changes that matter to a decision. A new observation can bring an earlier assumption back into focus.

Define what deserves attention, which evidence should accompany it, and who reviews the next step.

In practiceExample condition: an item’s cost rises above its realized selling price.

Structure you can see through is structure you can trust.

Five connected layers, built so that a value is recorded with its source and its dates, not merely described in a diagram.
