How your data is handled

From first connection to deletion.

Four stages, and the control that applies at each one.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Connections to your systems read what they hold. Your systems stay where they are and remain your systems of record.

    Read-only
  2. 02

    Keep

    Your data is stored in the United States, encrypted, and kept separate from every other client's.

    Separate and encrypted
  3. 03

    Answer

    Each answer points back to the record it came from, so your team can check it. Arthur asks for your approval before it sends a message on your behalf or changes a record in your systems.

    Sourced and approved
  4. 04

    Delete

    Ask us to delete your data and we remove it within 30 days and confirm in writing.

    Confirmed in writing

Plain about the edges

What to know before you send us anything.

  • Examples are illustrations. Interactive examples use demonstration data and fictional companies. They are not customer results.
  • The free Snapshot uses an AI model provider. To suggest how your columns map, the column names and a few sample values may be read by that provider.
  • Do not send secrets in a brief. No passwords or payment details, and nothing you are not allowed to share.
