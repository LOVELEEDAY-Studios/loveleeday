What happens to your data at each stage.

We read your systems and leave them in place.

Connections read what your systems hold. They remain your systems of record, and we write back only when you approve it.

We keep your data encrypted and separate.

Your data is kept on secure hosting in the United States, encrypted, and kept separate from every other client’s.

Every answer points back to its record.

Your team can check each answer against the record it came from. Where two sources disagree, you see both, and where a record is missing, the answer says so. Arthur asks for your approval before it sends a message on your behalf or changes a record in your systems.

We delete your data when you ask.

We remove it within 30 days of your request and confirm in writing.
