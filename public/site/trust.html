Trust

Your data stays yours.
And you can check that.

Kept separate, with core hosting and database in the United States, and deleted when you ask.

Connect

Connections to your systems read what they hold. Your systems stay where they are and remain your systems of record. Writing back happens only when you approve it.

Keep

Your data is kept on core hosting and a database in the United States, encrypted, and kept separate from every other client's.

Answer

Each answer points back to the record it came from, so your team can check it. Where two sources disagree, you see both. Where a record is missing, the answer says so.

Arthur asks for your approval before it sends a message on your behalf or changes a record in your systems.

Delete

Ask us to delete your data and we remove it within 30 days and confirm in writing.

This website

No cookies, no analytics and no tracking pixels. The privacy page lists each check. Private review links ask search engines not to index them, and an unrecognised link returns a not-found page.

Before you send us anything

Interactive examples use demonstration data and fictional companies. They are not customer results.

No passwords or payment details in a brief, and nothing you are not allowed to share.

Security and procurement

Your security team can review us. Ask for the security packet, written for security, legal and procurement teams, at hello@loveleedaystudios.com.
