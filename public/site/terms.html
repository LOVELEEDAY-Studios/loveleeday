Using the site

The site is information about LOVELEEDAY Studios LLC (“LOVELEEDAY”) and the way we work. You are welcome to read it, link to it and share it. Please do not copy its text, images or design as your own, and do not try to disrupt it.

Examples are illustrations

The interactive examples and sample records on this site use demonstration data. They show how a system can work; they are not verified customer results and not a promise of a particular outcome.

Sending a brief

Sending a project brief starts a conversation. It does not create a contract, an engagement or a duty to reply with a proposal. Any work begins only when both sides sign a written agreement for it.

Please do not send passwords, payment details or anything you are not allowed to share. How we handle what you do send is described on the privacy page.

Clients and the portal

Clients sign in to the client portal under a written agreement, and use of the portal is covered by its own terms and privacy page.

Limits

We work to keep the site accurate and available but provide it as is, with no promise it will always be up or free of mistakes. Links to other sites are for convenience; we are not responsible for them.

Changes and questions

We may update these terms and will change the date below when we do. Questions to hello@loveleedaystudios.com.
