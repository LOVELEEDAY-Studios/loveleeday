The data processing addendum.

The terms that apply when LOVELEEDAY handles personal information for a client.

Purpose and scope

This addendum applies when LOVELEEDAY Studios LLC (“LOVELEEDAY”) handles personal information on behalf of a client as part of an engagement. It forms part of the written agreement for that work. If it conflicts with the agreement on how personal information is handled, this addendum controls.

A signed copy is available on request at hello@loveleedaystudios.com.

Roles

The client decides why and how its personal information is used and is the controller (or, under California law, the business). LOVELEEDAY is the processor (or service provider) and handles that information only for the client.

Instructions

We process personal information only on the client’s documented instructions, which are the agreement, this addendum and the client’s use of the service. If we think an instruction breaks the law we will say so, and we may pause until it is settled.

What we process

Subject matter: reading the systems the client connects and answering questions from them. Duration: the length of the engagement plus the deletion period on the retention and deletion page. Data subjects: the client’s staff, customers, suppliers and others whose records sit in the connected systems. Types: whatever those systems hold, which the client scopes when it connects them.

Confidentiality

People at LOVELEEDAY who can reach client data are bound to keep it confidential and reach it only when the work needs it.

Security

We keep client data separate from every other client’s, encrypted, on hosting in the United States, and we limit access to those who need it. What we say about this is on the trust and security page; a longer security packet is available on request at hello@loveleedaystudios.com.

Subprocessors

The client agrees that we may use the subprocessors on the subprocessor list, and the ones named for its engagement. Each is bound to handle the information only to provide its service to us, with protections no weaker than these. We will tell the client in writing before adding or replacing a subprocessor that will handle its personal information, and the client may object on reasonable grounds; if we cannot resolve it, the client may end the affected work.

Requests from individuals

If someone asks us about information we hold for a client, we pass the request to the client and do not answer it ourselves unless the client tells us to. We help the client respond to access, correction, deletion and similar requests, to the extent the service makes that possible.

Personal data breach

If we confirm a breach of security that affects a client’s personal information, we will tell the client without undue delay, and within 72 hours of confirming it, with what we know about what happened, what was affected and what we are doing. We will keep the client informed as we learn more and help it meet its own notice duties.

Transfers

Client data is held in the United States. Where a client’s data comes from a place whose law restricts transfers, the parties rely on the standard contractual clauses approved for that purpose, which are incorporated here by reference, or another lawful mechanism.

Return and deletion

At the end of the engagement, or at any time the client asks, we return the client’s data in a common format and delete it, within 30 days of the request, and confirm in writing. The exceptions are in the retention and deletion page.

Use of data to improve our service

We do not use client data to improve our models or service except in the de-identified and aggregated way described in the AI and model improvement policy. A client may opt out in its agreement or by writing to us.

California service provider terms

We do not sell or share a client’s personal information, we do not keep, use or disclose it outside our direct business relationship with the client or for any purpose other than the services, and we do not combine it with information from other sources, except as the law allows. We will tell the client if we can no longer meet these duties.

Audits

We will give the client the information reasonably needed to show we are following this addendum, and will contribute to a reasonable audit, once a year or after a breach, at a time agreed in advance and under confidentiality.
