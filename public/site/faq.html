About LOVELEEDAY and Arthur

4 questions
What is LOVELEEDAY?

LOVELEEDAY is a studio in Kalamazoo, Michigan. We install and run Arthur, an AI operating system for organizations, and we build the software around it: custom applications, workflow automation, data migration, research, websites and brand work.

What is Arthur, and how is it different from LOVELEEDAY?

LOVELEEDAY is the company. Arthur is what we install and run for you. It reads the systems you connect, works out which records describe the same customer, supplier, item or document, keeps the history, and shows the source behind every figure.

Which industries do you work with?

Any organization that keeps its records in more than one place. Our examples cover manufacturing, property, local government, retail, hospitality, professional services, nonprofits and groups of companies. See the industries.

Are the examples on the site real?

They are illustrations. Interactive examples use demonstration data and fictional companies. They show how Arthur works and are not customer results.

02

Working with Arthur

4 questions
Do we have to replace our systems?

No. Your systems stay where they are and remain your systems of record. Arthur sits across them and reads what they hold.

Will Arthur change anything in our systems?

Connections are read-only. Arthur asks for your approval before it sends a message on your behalf or writes a record back.

How do we know an answer is right?

Each answer is built to show the record it came from, with its date. Where two sources disagree, Arthur shows both instead of choosing quietly. Where a record is missing, the answer says so.

Which systems can you connect to?

The integrations page lists the systems we are building connectors for, each marked with its status. If your system has an export, a database or an API, we can usually read it.

03

Getting started

4 questions
Where do we begin?

With one question and one system. The Intelligence Audit answers that question with its sources and gives you a written map of what else could be read. See how it works.

What does it cost?

We do not publish prices, because the right shape of work depends on your systems, your size and your question. Each engagement is scoped in a proposal written for you.

How quickly will you reply?

We aim to reply within one working day, in writing, from a person on our team.

Can I try something first?

Yes. The free price snapshot checks a price file you upload and shows the rows behind every finding. There is no account, and the file and result are deleted seven days after upload. To suggest how your columns map, the column names and a few sample values may be read by an AI model provider.

Your data

4 questions
Where is my data kept?

In the United States, encrypted, and kept separate from every other client's data.

Can I have my data deleted?

Yes. Ask us, and we remove it within 30 days and confirm in writing.

Do you have a security packet for our review team?

Yes. Write to us and we will send it. The trust center summarizes how client data is protected.

What does this website collect?

Only what you type into the project brief. There are no cookies and no analytics. The privacy page has the detail.

Still have a question?

Ask it in your own words. A person will answer in writing.
