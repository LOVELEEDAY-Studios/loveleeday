Use cases / Customer data

Many records.
A fuller customer picture.

Connect names, identifiers, purchases, and interactions. Keep uncertainty visible while you build a more reliable view.

Explore the records

Recognize the customer. Remember the context.

A customer can arrive through different channels with different spellings, account IDs, and contact information. Those records should contribute to the same understanding when the evidence supports a match.

Conflicting fields remain a review task. The goal is a useful customer picture with its sources intact.

Connect what belongs. Flag what needs care.

  • Normalize the recordsBring source fields into a consistent structure while preserving original values and identifiers.
  • Resolve the identityLink records using supporting evidence. Separate confirmed matches from candidates that need review.
  • Review data qualitySurface missing, stale, or conflicting information and retain a trail back to the original record.
