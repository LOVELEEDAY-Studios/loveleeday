A person or business is more than a row

Recognize the customer.
Remember the context.

A customer can arrive through different channels with different spellings, account IDs, and contact information. Those records should contribute to the same understanding when the evidence supports a match.

Conflicting fields remain a review task. The goal is one customer picture you can trust, where you can always see where each detail came from.

Every system remembers
a different part of the customer.

  • Sales remembers the promise.
  • Billing remembers the invoice.
  • Support remembers the complaint.

None of them is wrong. Together they are the customer, and only one view shows it.
