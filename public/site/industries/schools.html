Know what is due, and what proves it.

Reports for the authorizer, filings for the state, grant terms and staff records live in different places. LOVELEEDAY is designed to read them together and show what is due next, who has it, and the document that proves it was done.

01 ReadRead what you already keep

Enrollment and attendance reports, budgets, grant terms, staff files and board records are read from the places you keep them, scoped to what each person may see.

02 MatchSee each school as one place

A campus, a grant and a staff member tie back to one school and one record of truth, whichever system named them.

03 AnswerAnswer with the proof attached

Each due item shows the document that proves it is done, so a question from the authorizer has an answer the same day.

Single-site charter

One building, a small team

Charter network or management organization

District or independent school

Departments that each keep their own

Finance, enrollment, facilities and staffing each hold part of the answer. Bring them into one place before the board meeting or the accreditation visit.
