What LOVELEEDAY does here

Know what is due, who has it, and what proves it.

01 Read

Read what you already keep

Enrollment and attendance reports, budgets, grant terms, staff files and board records are read from the places you keep them, scoped to what each person may see.

02 Match

See each school as one place

A campus, a grant and a staff member tie back to one school and one record of truth, whichever system named them.

03 Answer

Answer with the proof attached

Each due item shows the document that proves it is done, so a question from the authorizer has an answer the same day.

Who it is for

The same questions, at three sizes of school.

Single-site charter

One building, a small team

The reports are the same ones a large district files, with far fewer people to do them. See the whole calendar of what you owe, and which person on your small team has each item.

Charter network or management organization

Many campuses, one set of rules

Each campus keeps its records a little differently. See which campus is behind, compare them fairly, and give the board one picture.

District or independent school

Departments that each keep their own

Finance, enrollment, facilities and staffing each hold part of the answer. Bring them into one place before the board meeting or the accreditation visit.

Systems it connects to

Works with where your records already live.

Boards, school leaders and business offices see the same figures, scoped to what each is allowed to read.
