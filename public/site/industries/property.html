Before you send the renewal letter.

If we raise rent 8% on a tenant who has been with us four years, what could it really cost us?

An estimate for one example unit, with illustrative figures. Sources: lease and ledger, vacancy and turnover history.

$1,440more rent over the year, if they stay.
$4,500 to $6,500lost if they leave: two to three empty months plus turnover costs.
  • 8% on a $1,500 rent$120 a month
  • Twelve months of the increase$1,440
  • If they leave2 to 3 empty months plus turnover

A smaller increase, such as 3%, is worth weighing: it asks for less and lowers the chance of an empty unit.
