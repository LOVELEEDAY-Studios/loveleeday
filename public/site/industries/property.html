What LOVELEEDAY does here

Know which property needs the first call, and why.

01 Read

Read your platform

Tenant ledgers, leases, work orders and vendor records are read from the platform you manage from, scoped to what each person may see.

02 Match

See each building as one place

A unit, a lease and a work order tie back to one building and one owner of record, whichever system named them.

03 Answer

Answer with sources

Each flagged building shows the ledger and work-order records behind it, so the first call goes to the right place.
