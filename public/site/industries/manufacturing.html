Every system a part touches, in one place

When a part is late or on hold, see the orders it affects, the customers waiting, and the records behind the answer. LOVELEEDAY is designed to read your ERP, purchasing and quality records so you do not have to piece it together.

A part with four names should have one record

Your parts list, purchasing records, inspection log and quotes call the same part by different names. LOVELEEDAY shows it as one part, so what is on hand, on order, on hold and promised to customers reads as one picture, with the source of each fact beside it.

Your systems stay the source of truth.

Read access first. Nothing is written back to any system without an approval from someone you name.

  • Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business CentralReads customers, vendors, sales invoices and purchase invoices. Read-only.
  • Microsoft Dynamics 365 (Finance and Operations)Reads customers, vendors, sales orders and invoices. Read-only.
  • Epicor Prophet 21Reads customers, invoices and orders. Read-only.
  • SAP Business OneReads business partners, invoices, orders and items. Read-only.
  • Microsoft 365 (Graph: Outlook, SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams, Excel)Reads Outlook mail, SharePoint libraries, OneDrive files and Teams messages. Read-only.
  • CSV and Excel uploadReads a spreadsheet or CSV you send us, mapped to your columns.

Status describes our plan for each system, not a live connection. None is presented here as connected for any customer. Current status for every system is on the integrations page.
