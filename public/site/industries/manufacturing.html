What LOVELEEDAY does here

Three steps from your records to a figure you can defend.

01 Read

Read your systems

Item master, price lists, invoices and customer history are read from the systems that already hold them. The ERP stays the system of record.

02 Resolve

Resolve to one item

A part number, a supplier SKU and a quote line become one item, so costs and prices line up across sources.

03 Answer

Answer with sources

Each margin gap names the invoice or price list it came from, so the team can check it before changing a price.

Systems it connects to
