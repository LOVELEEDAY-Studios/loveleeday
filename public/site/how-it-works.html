From first question to a working answer.

Every project follows the same path. You bring a question about your data and a system that holds part of it. We answer it with the source behind every figure, and show you what else could be read. At each step, you decide whether to continue. Below, the four steps, each shown with the portal screen it uses.

Start a projectSee the four engagements

The path from first conversation to ongoing partnership

  1. 01

    Tell us the question

    Send a short brief: the decision you need to make, the records you have, and what a useful result would look like. We reply in writing, aiming for one working day.

  2. 02

    Intelligence Audit

    We connect one system, read-only, and answer one question with the source behind every figure. You receive a written map of what else could be read and our recommendation, whether or not you continue.

  3. 03

    30-Day Deployment

    Your team can ask across its core systems, check the records behind each answer, and approve actions from one workspace. We set up access and train the people who will use it, and you keep a handover record.

  4. 04

    Intelligence Partner

    LOVELEEDAY runs alongside your team as your systems and questions change: connection health, new questions and reports each month, and a quarterly review of decisions and outcomes.

You keep the decisions while we do the connecting

You tell us the question and give us read access to the systems involved. We connect them, work out which records describe the same customer, supplier, item or document, and keep the source behind every answer.

Arthur asks for your approval before it sends a message on your behalf or changes a record in your systems. Your systems stay your systems of record.

Something you can use, whether or not you continue.

Every engagement ends with something in your hands: an answer with its sources, a written map of what else could be read, a handover record, and an honest recommendation.

We do not publish prices, because the right shape of work depends on your systems, your size and your question. Each engagement is scoped in a proposal written for you.
