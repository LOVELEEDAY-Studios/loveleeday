The path from first conversation to ongoing partnership

Tell us the question

Send a short brief: the decision you need to make, the records you have, and what a useful result would look like. We reply in writing, aiming for one working day.

Intelligence Audit

We connect one system, read-only, and answer one question with the source behind every figure. You receive a written map of what else could be read and our recommendation, whether or not you continue.

30-Day Deployment

Your team can ask across its core systems, check the records behind each answer, and approve actions from one workspace. We set up access and train the people who will use it, and you keep a handover record.

Intelligence Partner

LOVELEEDAY runs alongside your team as your systems and questions change: connection health, new questions and reports each month, and a quarterly review of decisions and outcomes.

You keep the decisions while we do the connecting

You tell us the question and give us read access to the systems involved. We connect them, work out which records describe the same customer, supplier, item or document, and keep the source behind every answer. Arthur asks for your approval before it sends a message on your behalf or changes a record in your systems. Your systems stay your systems of record.
