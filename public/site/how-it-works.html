to a working answer.

Every project follows the same path. You bring a question about your data and a system that holds part of it. We answer it with the source behind every figure, and show you what else could be read. At each step, you decide whether to continue. Below, one example followed through all four steps.

Who does what

You keep the decisions.
We do the connecting.

You tell us the question and give us read access to the systems involved. We connect them, work out which records describe the same customer, supplier, item or document, and keep the source behind every answer.

Arthur asks for your approval before it sends a message on your behalf or changes a record in your systems. Your systems stay your systems of record.

What you keep

Something you can use,
whether or not you continue.

Every engagement ends with something in your hands: an answer with its sources, a written map of what else could be read, a handover record, and an honest recommendation.

We do not publish prices, because the right shape of work depends on your systems, your size and your question. Each engagement is scoped in a proposal written for you.
