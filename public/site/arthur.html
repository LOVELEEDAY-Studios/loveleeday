Arthur establishes the facts before it answers.

First it determines which customer, supplier or document the question refers to, what was true at the time, and what the records can support.

Identifies the subject

Arthur recognizes when different names and account numbers refer to the same customer, company or item.

Follows the record

It connects related events in the order they happened and keeps the source of each one.

Flags uncertainty

Confirmed facts, estimates and open questions are labeled separately, so you know what you can rely on.

Complex questions are answered step by step.

Arthur breaks a complex question into the smaller questions it depends on, then answers each one with the supporting record attached.
