What do we connect first?

One system and one decision. We connect it read-only, so nothing in it can change, and answer your question from there.

Yes. Each figure leads back to the record behind it, so anyone on your team can check it.

What happens when two records disagree?

You see both, and where each one came from. Someone on your team decides which is right. Nothing gets overwritten along the way.

No. Your systems stay where they are. We work from what they already hold.
