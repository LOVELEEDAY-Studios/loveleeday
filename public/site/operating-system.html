Common questions

What do we connect first?

We start with one system and one decision. The connection is read-only, so nothing in that system can change.

Does every answer show its source?

Yes. Each figure links to the record behind it, so anyone on your team can verify it.

What happens when records disagree?

We show both records and where each came from. Your team decides which is correct, and nothing is overwritten.

Do we need to replace our existing systems?

No. Your systems stay in place. LOVELEEDAY works from the information they already hold.
