Before you begin

A useful starting point.

What do we connect first?

One decision and the sources behind it. We agree the question, who owns each record, and what a complete answer would include before anything is connected.

Does an answer include its evidence?

Yes. Every answer is built to lead back to the record it came from, with its date, so your team can check it.

What happens when sources disagree?

Arthur keeps both and shows them side by side. A disagreement becomes something to review, not a value that is quietly overwritten.

Does this replace every existing system?

No. Your systems stay where they are and remain the source of truth. Arthur reads them, read-only, and works across them. It asks for your approval before it writes anything back.
