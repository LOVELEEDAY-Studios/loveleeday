We started LOVELEEDAY to give people back hours in their day

Owners and managers make decisions every week without the full picture, because gathering it takes too long. The answers are usually sitting in sales, accounting, scheduling, email and documents, and very few teams have someone whose job is to put them together. That is the job we took on. LOVELEEDAY was founded by Daniel May.

More time for the rest of your day.

Time to look up from the screen, notice the world around you, and spend it with the people and places you love.

What we kept seeing

  • 01

    Decisions pile up faster than answers.

    People spend days gathering numbers for a choice that takes ten minutes once the facts are in front of them.

  • 02

    Small teams have more to manage than they have people.

    A twenty-person organization faces the same suppliers, rules and reporting as a large one, without analysts to keep up.

  • 03

    Every system holds a piece.

    Sales, accounting and scheduling each keep their own records, so someone has to put the whole picture together by hand.

LOVELEEDAY is the company. Arthur is what we run for you.

Arthur reads the systems you already use and answers in plain language: what happened, why it matters and what to do next, with the source behind every figure so you can check it. You keep the decision, because Arthur asks before it sends a message or changes a record.

What we promise

  • Every figure points back to its record

    You can follow any number to the system and the line it came from.

  • Earlier records stay

    New information adds to the history. It never overwrites it.

  • Gaps are shown, not hidden

    Where sources disagree or a record is missing, the answer says so.

  • You keep the decision

    Arthur asks for your approval before it sends a message or changes a record.
