Use cases / Operational intelligence

Know what changed and what to do next.

When something changes in one part of your business, see who and what it affects before the next decision is due.

The answer crosses more than one team.

A rise in open work orders can involve a site, a contractor, and a budget. An overdue invoice can connect a customer, a support ticket, and a contract renewal.

The first step is to define how these things relate. Then every question that crosses teams has evidence behind it and someone who can review it.

One change can widen the view

What changed

Open work orders rise at one site.

New work-order records arrive, with their source and dates.

Context

Related records come into view.

The site connects to its contractor, its assets and its service agreement.

Attention

You get the answer and the next step.

Repairs are rising at this site. Check whether the contractor and service agreement can cover the backlog, then assign an owner to the delayed work.
