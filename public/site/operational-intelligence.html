One change reaches further than it looks

A rise in repairs involves a site, a contractor and a budget. An overdue invoice touches a customer, a support ticket and a contract renewal. No single team sees all of it. Arthur follows the connections for you and hands each question to the person who can act, with the evidence attached.

It asks the smaller questions you would

Give Arthur one plain question. It works out what it needs to check first, checks each piece against your records, and answers with where every piece came from.

Someone asks

If this part ships late, which customers wait?

First it checks

Which open orders need the part?

Then

Which customers placed them?

Then

Did the latest batch pass inspection?

Finally

What did the supplier say last?

Arthur answers

Fourteen orders wait on it, and the latest batch failed inspection. Call those customers first.
