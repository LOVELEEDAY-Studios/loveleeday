We collect very little

What this website collects, which services handle it, and how to ask us to delete what you send.

What this website collects

The contact form and the project brief on the studio page ask for your name, your email and your organization, what you are writing about, the question you need answered, and, if you choose, the systems or files that hold the answer. Name, email, organization and the question are required.

The project brief

Your brief is emailed to hello@loveleedaystudios.com and a confirmation is emailed to you.

Where a form offers you a copy, it downloads to your own device, so you keep what you sent.

We do not keep the brief in a database. The copies are the two emails and any copy downloaded to your device.

Who else handles it

We use three services to host the site and handle email, and one more for the free pricing Snapshot.

  • Vercel hosts the site and keeps standard server request logs.
  • Resend delivers the two emails.
  • Purelymail hosts the inbox the brief arrives in.
  • For the Snapshot only, an outside language-processing provider may read the column names and a few sample values to suggest how your columns map. The file and result are deleted seven days after upload.

Cookies and analytics

  • We do not use cookies or browser storage on this website.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels or advertising tags.
  • We load no tracking or advertising scripts and no externally hosted fonts.
  • We do not profile you, make automated decisions about you or sell your information.

Links we share with you

We sometimes share work through a private link that opens without signing in. We ask search engines not to list or preview these pages. Anyone with the link can open the page, so share it only with people you want to give access.

Keeping and deleting what you send

To ask us to delete the brief and confirmation emails we hold, write to us. To ask for deletion, email hello@loveleedaystudios.com, and we will remove both the brief and the confirmation from our mailbox within 30 days and confirm in writing. You can delete the downloaded copy from your device.

Questions

For privacy questions, email hello@loveleedaystudios.com.
