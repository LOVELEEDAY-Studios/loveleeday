Use cases / Pricing & margins

See what sells.
Know what it leaves.

Review actual prices alongside item costs, discounts, and variable fees. Find where the economics need a closer look.

Explore the numbers

Follow the money. Item by item.

A listed price can look healthy while discounts and delivery costs tell a different story. Connect the price a customer actually pays with the costs attached to that sale.

A margin review makes those assumptions explicit. It identifies prices to investigate and keeps proposed changes tied to their inputs.

See the margin move.

Change the inputs to explore a single item’s contribution margin. Amounts use dollars for this example.

Realized revenue = listed price × (1 − discount). Contribution = realized revenue − item cost − other variable costs. The target price keeps the same discount. This example excludes fixed overhead and taxes; add per-unit costs relevant to your review.

From one item. To a reviewable plan.

  • Reconcile the inputsMatch catalogue items to cost records, selling prices, discounts, and relevant variable costs.
  • Flag the exceptionsIdentify losses, margins below the chosen target, and missing or outdated cost observations.
  • Propose the changesPrepare item-level changes with assumptions, the records behind them, and an opportunity to review before anything is written back.
