A healthy list price can hide a thin margin.

Discounts and delivery costs can change the result of a sale that looks fine at list price. We connect the price a customer paid with the costs attached to that sale.

A margin review states each assumption openly, identifies the prices to investigate and ties every proposed change to its inputs.
