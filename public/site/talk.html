Working together

Four ways to begin.
One conversation to choose.

LOVELEEDAY installs and runs Arthur in four ways, from a short audit of one system to a long-term partnership. We do not publish prices, because the right shape of work depends on your systems, your size and the question you need answered. Each engagement is scoped in a proposal written for you.

Start here

The four engagements

Intelligence Audit

A short, fixed look at one system and one question.

  • One system connected read-only
  • One question answered with its sources
  • A written map of what else could be read
  • A recommendation, whether or not you continue
Start with an Audit
Build

30-Day Deployment

Your core systems connected and a working workspace for your team.

  • Up to six systems connected and verified
  • Entities and locations modeled
  • Roles, approvals and sign-in configured
  • Team training and a handover record
Plan a deployment
Ongoing

Intelligence Partner

LOVELEEDAY runs alongside your team as systems and questions change.

  • Continuous connection health and freshness
  • New questions and reports every month
  • Approvals inbox monitored with your team
  • Quarterly review of decisions and outcomes
Talk about a partnership

Founding Client Program

  • Priority on connectors you need
  • Joint roadmap sessions each quarter
