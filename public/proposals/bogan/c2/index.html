Housing development
Michigan

Bogan Developments builds affordable and workforce housing in Michigan, and keeps the record where anyone can read it.

Minority-led and founded in 2020, the firm builds where a community already has a street, a school and a story, and needs homes to go with them. The work runs from reclaimed brownfield in Northside Kalamazoo to a healthcare campus and thirteen acres in South Haven Township, always with named public and private partners.

The Standard

Five words, and the record that answers each.

  1. EEffort

    Zone 32 Phase I went up with no investors, on debt its founder personally signed for.

  2. LLeadership

    Developer of Zone 32 and The B on Burdick, and development partner at Lighthouse Ridge: 187 homes across four communities, delivered, under construction or planned.

  3. IImpact

    A 1.4-acre brownfield on N. Pitcher Street became twelve homes and an early learning center. Phase II reclaims 0.6 acre more.

  4. TTeamwork

    A health system, a state agency, a tribal investment arm and an architect, each named on the work: Bronson Healthcare, MSHDA, the Pokagon Band’s Mno-Bmadsen and TowerPinkster.

  5. EExecution

    Phase I is delivered. Phase II broke ground on September 10, 2026, and targets fall 2027.

The founder

No investors. His own name on the debt.

A New Jersey native and a former Western Michigan University running back who wore number 32, Jamauri Bogan finished his MBA at WMU in 2019 and founded Bogan Developments a year later. He rose from intern to partner at Edison Community Partners. On the first phase of Zone 32 he had no investors and personally signed off on the debt.

His leadership philosophy fits on one line: “build people, and the projects will follow.”

Michelle Audette-Bauman, Vice President, brings more than twenty years in development, including seven at the Michigan Economic Development Corporation and the lead developer role on a $30.5 million mixed-use brownfield project.

“A complete neighborhood is walkable, has access to groceries, a place to get your haircut, housing, and neighborhood amenities—paired with improved infrastructure where people can live, work, and play.”Jamauri Bogan, Founder and CEO
