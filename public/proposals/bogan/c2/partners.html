Cities and funders

Brownfield parcels returned to use, and public money named project by project.

Housing as part of the workforce plan.

Bronson Healthcare, one of the region’s anchor employers, is a partner in a planned five-story building inside its own Healthy Living Campus. Bronson owns the parcels and will lease 20 of the 85 homes for its employees.

The plan pairs $2 million from the MSHDA Employer-Assisted Housing Program with $2 million from the Kalamazoo County Housing Millage. Bogan Developments will own the building and engage a management company. Rents are set for households at 60 to 120 percent of area median income, and 33 percent of construction work is earmarked for minority- and women-owned firms.
