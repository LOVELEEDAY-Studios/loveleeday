# Round 3: Editorial Luxe — "The Long Table Issue"

## Concept
A holiday issue of a design magazine. Cover with a 15.6vw Fraunces masthead title overlapping the cover photo, an editor's letter, a dark "In this issue" contents list where the four products are articles (hover reveals a floating photo plate, + adds to bag), a full-bleed plate with a pull quote, a "The hands behind it" artists feature, the Family Set as a still life with four hotspots, a back page for delivery, and a colophon with a giant italic wordmark. The gift-tag device (clipped corner, punched hole) marks the cover price and the Dec 7 stamps. Working bag drawer with free-shipping progress at $59.

## References (Dribbble, refs/sheet.jpg)
1. "Magazine" OAD (luxury-magazine-website-04): oversize serif title beside a tiny sans deck, hairline article rows -> contents list and masthead meta line.
2. Sandbay (minimal-product-website-serif-03/05): tight-tracked high-contrast serif over warm paper -> cover scale, ink/pomegranate blocking.
3. Lou Leisure (editorial-ecommerce-website-01): numbered index with hairlines, overlapping asymmetric plates -> numbered contents, offset second image in the artists feature.

## Type and color
Fraunces opsz 144, weight 300, tracking -0.045em (italic SOFT/WONK for the pomegranate accent word); Instrument Sans tiny tracked labels; Shantell Sans only on tags. Paper #F3ECE0, ink #1E1420, accent #B8324A.

## Weaknesses
- The set is not one light: dark red studio cover, candlelit plate, soft-daylight flatlay and cutout. Cohesion comes from palette (red, teal, ochre, cream), not one photo setup.
- hands-wrapping is 1024px wide and soft at full bleed; the cover is a crop of a landscape image.
- No extreme paper/ribbon macros anymore, so the magazine "texture" beat is missing.
- Motion is restrained (reveals, parallax, hover plate, hotspots); no pinned GSAP-grade sequences.
