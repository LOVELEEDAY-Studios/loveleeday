- Hue-shifting the gift also shifts its drawn skin tones slightly; real colourway photos are needed.
- Mobile hero: gift and roll are small and crowd the headline edges.
- UGC slots are flat colour blocks; the phone frame is a still photo.
- Tight display tracking is borderline on some word pairs.
- Chromium only; Safari/Firefox untested.
