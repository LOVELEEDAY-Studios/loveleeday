Open any answer and see where it came from.

Five checks run underneath every one. Watch what each does to the field.

  1. 01

    Names

    Different names, one customer

    A customer can be a name in your inbox, a number in your ledger and a nickname in conversation. Arthur learns they are one, so everything about them stays together.

  2. 02

    Sources

    Every answer has a way back

    Each fact Arthur uses keeps a note of the system and record it came from. If it cannot say where something came from, it does not use it.

  3. 03

    History

    What you knew, and when

    Arthur remembers when something was true and when you found out. You can look back at a decision with what you knew at the time, without losing anything learned since.

  4. 04

    Checks

    Checked before it is said

    Before an answer goes out, every number in it is checked against your records. Anything it cannot support is held back.

  5. 05

    Changes

    Told when something changes

    Arthur keeps an eye on the facts behind your decisions. When one of them changes, it brings the decision back to you.
