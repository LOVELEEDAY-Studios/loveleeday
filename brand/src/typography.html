05 Typography

Two faces, one clear rule.

Sentient speaks for the brand: the question, the statement, the quote. Switzer informs: the interface, the body, the data. Pick by the job the words do, and the choice is never a debate. New in 2.0

5.2 Pairing logic

The job of the words picks the face.

Set the same line both ways and the difference is clear. A question sounds human in Sentient. A data label is faster to scan in Switzer.

Line one: a question a person would ask
Which customers are buying less this quarter?
Correct: Sentient 400. The brand is speaking, so it gets the voice face.
Which customers are buying less this quarter?
Acceptable, flatter: Switzer 400. Fine inside a small control, but it loses the voice.
Line two: a label on a data view
Revenue by customer, last 90 days
Correct: Switzer 400. The reader is scanning, so it gets the quiet face.
Revenue by customer, last 90 days
Wrong: Sentient. A serif in a table or label reads as decoration and slows the scan.

Sentient is correct when

The brand is speaking to a person: the hero statement, an answer card's question, a pull quote, a customer line. One Sentient line per screen is the norm. Never below 22 px.

Switzer is correct when

The page is informing: navigation, body, buttons, labels, tables, numbers, forms, headings of sections. Everything the person has to read, compare or click.

5.4 Rules

Nine rules, each shown both ways.

Most type problems come from the same few habits. Each rule below pairs the right way with the version to avoid.

1. Hold the measure at 60 to 75 characters

About 66 is ideal. A wide line makes the eye lose its place.

The best answer is the one you can act on today. Keep each line short enough that the eye returns to the next one without effort.

Minimum sizes

Marketing site: body never below 16 px, and no text below 13 px anywhere. Product: table cells 13 px, field labels and timestamps 12 px, and never for sentences. Buttons and tap targets are at least 44 px tall.

Contrast rules

Normal text needs 4.5 : 1 and large text (24 px, or 19 px bold) needs 3 : 1, per WCAG 2.1 AA. Ratios above are on cream #FBF8F2. Dim on the sunk ground #F2EDE3 falls to 4.51 : 1, so keep it off that ground below 14 px. Teal on navy is 2.95 : 1: never text.

Grounding: hex values from the :root block of this book; ratios computed with the WCAG 2.1 relative luminance formula on 2026-10-18.
