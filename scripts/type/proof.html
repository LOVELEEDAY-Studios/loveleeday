One colour, real outlines, measured spacing.

The O keeps the typeface’s own outer contour; only its counter is replaced. The heart is sized so the ring left around it equals that face’s measured stem width, so the letter does not read lighter than its neighbours. Spacing is not a table of guessed numbers: the facing ink profiles of every adjacent pair are sampled across their shared height and the advance is solved for a constant average gap, with a floor that makes a collision impossible. Each mark is shown at display size, then at 30/18/11px, then reversed.
